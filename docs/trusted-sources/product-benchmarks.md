# Product benchmarks

Last checked: 2026-08-07

Benchmark sources provide observed product precedent: they help Design Arc inspect how relevant complete journeys address comparable choices, transitions, and non-happy states. They are used only in Benchmarks mode when the user has separately authorized access and the journey is actually inspected.

## What precedent can support

[Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/) is an external benchmark provider that can make real-product journeys available for inspection. A relevant, end-to-end inspected journey can inform a reasoned comparison and expose patterns worth considering against the user-confirmed objective.

Benchmark precedent is not a first-party requirement, a product endorsement, or automatic proof of quality. A library listing, popularity signal, metadata, or isolated screenshot does not establish that a pattern is best-in-class, complete, suitable for this product, or accessible.

## Access and limits

Mobbin access is separate from Design Arc and is never bundled, copied, mirrored, or offered for download by this repository. If authorized access is unavailable, Design Arc stops and offers the explicitly chosen Guidelines fallback or a confirmed saved switch; it does not silently claim benchmark evidence. Guidelines mode performs no benchmark lookup and makes no benchmark-evidence claim.

The resulting recommendation remains Design Arc's clearly labeled product judgment, tested against current first-party platform guidance rather than presented as a benchmark-provider conclusion.
