# Design Arc privacy notice

Effective: 17 August 2026

[Home](../README.md) · [Trust and sources](trust-limitations-and-sources.md) · [Terms](terms.md) · [Support](support.md)

Design Arc is a skills-only design-review plugin. Design Arc does not operate a developer-controlled backend and does not independently collect, transmit, sell, or monetize personal data.

## Information handled during use

Your AI coding platform may read the product materials that you deliberately place in the review. Design Arc may direct the platform to create project-local preferences, review records, evidence notes, journey boards, and graph records under the runtime-specific `.codex/`, `.claude/`, or `.gemini/` paths documented in this repository.

Those files remain under the project owner’s control. Whether they are committed, shared, retained, or deleted is controlled by the user and the project’s own tooling.

## AI coding platform and external services

Use of Codex, Claude Code, Google Antigravity, GitHub, Mobbin, Google Stitch, official-guidance websites, browsers, or other tools is governed by those providers’ terms and privacy practices. External services remain separately authorized. Design Arc requires your AI coding platform to identify the intended service and obtain the necessary permission before sending project material outside the current environment.

Design Arc does not bundle credentials, OAuth access, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, an MCP server, or a hosted database.

## Support

Public support requests can be filed through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/friedbeef1/design-arc/issues). Do not include credentials, private product data, personal information, or confidential review evidence in a public issue.

Material privacy changes will be documented in this repository with an updated effective date.
